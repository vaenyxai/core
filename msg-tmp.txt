fix(ui): repair the text I double-encoded in styles.css

The sidebar showed a mangled glyph where a disclosure triangle belongs.
My fault, and traceable: a PowerShell one-liner used to swap a radius
token read styles.css as Windows ANSI and wrote it back as UTF-8,
double-encoding every non-ASCII character in the file. Comments merely
looked ugly; two lines mattered, because they are content:
  content: "▸"  and  content: "▾"
- which is what Oskar saw in front of Testing and Unsorted.

Repaired by the exact inverse: encode each character back to its CP1252
byte, then read those bytes as UTF-8. CP1252, not Latin-1 - the em dash,
the ellipsis and the curly quotes all live in 0x80..0x9F, where the two
differ, and that was most of the damage. A line was only accepted when
the repair strictly reduced its high-character count, so the legitimate
lone characters in the file (§ in a spec reference, × on a close button)
were left as they are.

68 lines restored; 7 verified correct already.

Lesson worth the commit message: never pipe a source file through
PowerShell Get-Content/Set-Content. Read and Edit, or a node script that
states its encoding.

Bump 0.3.1-dev.54.
