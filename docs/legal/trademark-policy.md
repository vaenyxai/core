1.3 This Policy governs use of the **brand only**. Use of the Vaenyx **software and source code** is governed by the applicable code licence and the Vaenyx Terms of Service, not by this Policy (see clause 4).

## 8. Naming Rules for User Content

8.1 User Content may be described as being **"for Vaenyx"** — for example, *"Invoice Sorter for Vaenyx"* or *"A meal-planning Routine for Vaenyx"*.

8.2 User Content must **not** be named as if it were official or made by us. Do not:
  (a) name an item so that "Vaenyx" is its first word (for example *"Vaenyx Invoice Sorter"*) — leading with the mark implies official origin;
  (b) use the Vaenyx logo or icon as, or within, the item's own icon or imagery; or
  (c) describe an item as "official", "certified", "verified", "trusted", or "endorsed" unless we have designated it as such in writing. A Verified status displayed by the Community Library permits use of the word **"Verified"** for that item and that version only; it is a moderation-status label meaning the item passed our automated checks, not a statement that the item is official, certified, trusted, or endorsed by us, and it does not modify the Terms of Service provisions on Community Content (Terms of Service clauses 8.2 and 8.4).

8.3 In short: a name in the form *"Something for Vaenyx"* is fine; *"Vaenyx Something"* is not.

8.4 This clause operates alongside any naming and moderation rules the Community Library applies from time to time. Compliance with the library's automated checks does not by itself amount to compliance with this Policy, and we may require renaming of published items that breach it.
