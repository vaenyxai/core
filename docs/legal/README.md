# Vaenyx — Legal Documents

The legal documents that govern your use of Vaenyx and its Community Library.

**Current set: v3.1 (27 July 2026)** — in-app notices pack at copy version 2.8.

The set has three layers. The **core documents** state the durable rules and do not change when a
feature ships. The **in-app notices** are what you are actually shown at the moment of a decision.
The **Schedule** is the factual record of what is built today and what data actually moves.

**What this release does, in plain terms.** Vaenyx runs on your own computer. This release has no
community-sharing upload, no attaching of your examples to anything you publish, and no Merit. A
sharing setting you choose now records a preference only — it does not authorise any upload. If a
capability like that arrives later, it stays off until you are shown current information and make a
fresh choice. The Schedule is the place to check what is true today; it describes what exists and
can never authorise anything beyond these documents.

The Operator is the entity identified in the Terms of Service. You can reach the Operator at hello@vaenyx.ai.
