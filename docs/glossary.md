## The Basics
- **Vaenyx** — Your own private AI helper that runs on your home computer. Your data stays on your machine.
- **Chat** — A conversation with Vaenyx. A chat can also do work (run something, on a schedule); a small status light shows what it's doing.
- **Project** — A folder that groups related chats and the things Vaenyx should remember for them.
- **Unsorted** — Where new chats go until you file them into a Project. (It is not itself a Project.)

## Things You Run
- **Routine** — The thing you tap to get something done (e.g. "Family Ledger", "What's for Dinner"). This is what you use day to day. Built from one or more Methods.
- **Method** — A single building-block skill (e.g. "read a receipt"). Creators combine Methods into Routines. You rarely touch these directly.
- **Journal** — A Routine's record of everything you put in (e.g. every receipt you snapped). Stored on your device only.
- **Gallery** — A Routine's saved results (e.g. each monthly summary it made). Open it anytime — no need to redo the work. On your device only.

## Finding & Sharing
- **Library** — The in-app store where you find and add Routines, organized by everyday area (money, kids, health, food, home).
- **Verified** — A Routine or creator that has been checked. You see Verified by default; switch on Community to see everything.
- **Merit** — A creator's trust score. It rises when their Routines are genuinely useful and people thank them — not from being popular.
- **Creator** — Someone who creates and shares Routines or Methods. You need no account to use Vaenyx; you only sign in if you want to publish.

## How a Routine Runs
- **Manual** — It runs only when you ask (the default).
- **Auto-run (schedule)** — It runs on its own, daily or weekly, at a set time. "Daily" means each calendar day (resets at midnight) — not 24 hours after you turned it on.
- **On an event** *(coming later)* — It runs when something happens.

## About You & Limits
- **Vaenyx Me** — your digital self. A private, growing picture of who you are, owned and shaped by you alone. The more you give it, the more Vaenyx can think and act the way you would — until, over time, it becomes something rare: a piece of you that's truly your own to keep.
- **Memory** — What Vaenyx remembers, kept per Project. You can see and edit it.
- **Custom Mode** — A restricted mode with rules you set (allowed topics, time limits, locked settings).
